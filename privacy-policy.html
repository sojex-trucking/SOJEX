Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how SOJEX handles information submitted through this website and the carrier application. Last updated September 7, 2026.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily submit through the carrier application form, including:

  • Name and contact information
  • Company information
  • MC/DOT information
  • Truck and equipment information
  • Number and type of trucks
  • Preferred lanes
  • Dispatcher-related information
  • Any other information you choose to provide

2. How We Use Information

SOJEX may use submitted information to contact carriers, review carrier applications, provide and manage dispatch services, communicate regarding the 14-day free trial, communicate about SOJEX services, improve the website and services, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Formspree / Third-Party Services

SOJEX uses third-party services, including Formspree, to process website form submissions. Information submitted through the carrier application form may therefore be processed by these third-party service providers according to their respective privacy practices. Please review those providers’ privacy policies for more information.

4. Information Sharing

SOJEX does not sell or rent personal information. We may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide services, or when required by law.

5. Data Security

SOJEX takes reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

SOJEX may retain submitted information for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, service provision, recordkeeping, legal, or legitimate business purposes.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Where applicable, you may contact SOJEX to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact information coming soon.

8. Cookies and Tracking

SOJEX does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, or other tracking technologies on this website. Third-party services used for form submission may process technical information according to their own privacy practices.

9. Children’s Privacy

This website and SOJEX services are not directed toward children under 13.

10. Policy Updates

SOJEX may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this website.
