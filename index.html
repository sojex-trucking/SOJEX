Questions, answered

Good to
know.

Clear expectations make for better partnerships.

What is the Sojex free trial?+

New carriers can try Sojex dispatch support completely free for 14 days.

Is there a dispatch fee during the trial?+

No. There is no dispatch fee during the 14-day trial.

What happens after the 14-day trial?+

Sojex will discuss continuation options with the carrier.

Do I have to accept every load?+

No. The carrier remains in control of which loads they choose to haul.

What types of carriers do you work with?+

Sojex focuses on owner-operators, independent carriers, and small trucking companies.

Do you negotiate rates?+

Yes. Rate negotiation is one of Sojex’s dispatch support services.

Do you guarantee loads or profits?+

No. Sojex does not guarantee specific loads, revenue, or profits.

How do I start?+

Complete the free-trial application and Sojex will contact you about getting started.
